# Release v14.5.0

## Feature — The astral plane on the canvas

Astral perception and astral projection now change what a token sees on the scene, and who can see it.

### Astral abilities come from feats

Feats of type Awakened, Power or Trait have an **Astral plane** section with four checkboxes:

- **Astral perception**: the character can switch to astral perception.
- **Astral projection**: the character can leave its body (includes perception).
- **Dual nature**: present on both planes, perceives the astral plane permanently (ghoul, vampire, shapeshifter…).
- **Astral being**: spirit or elemental, switches between materialised and astral form.

The character sheet only offers the states the character can enter. A character without any of these abilities no longer shows the astral badge, and a character that loses the feat while on the astral plane comes back to physical.

### What a perceiving token sees

When the selected token perceives the astral plane (astral perception, astral form, dual nature):

- The map is tinted.
- Other tokens show what anyone sees at a glance, without an astral observation test:
  - a living being that is only physical has a faint, slowly undulating aura. Awakened and ordinary beings look the same;
  - a being active on the astral plane (astral perception, astral form of a projection, dual nature, spirit) shows as a much stronger, brighter shape;
  - what is not alive (vehicles, drones, ICE) has no aura and blends into the scenery.
- The system's generic tokens (a figure on a white disc) are cut out, so the aura follows the figure and not the disc.
- Astral sight is blocked by anything solid: walls, doors and windows.

### Astral projection and astral beings

- Projecting leaves the body token in place and creates an **astral form** token next to it. Deleting the form or the body, or switching state from either sheet, ends the projection.
- A spirit or elemental in astral form keeps its own token. Materialised, it behaves as a dual-natured being.
- Astral forms move through walls, shed no light on the physical plane and are hidden from viewers who do not perceive the astral plane. A GM with no token selected sees them all.
- Leaving the physical plane disconnects the character from the Matrix.

### Optional — Essence shown in auras

A new world setting, **Auras: show the loss of Essence (house rule)**, is off by default. By the rules, reading the Essence of an aura takes an astral observation test. As a house rule, turning it on makes auras darken and show dark blotches below Essence 6, down to black at 0.

### Migration

Creatures already in a world carry these abilities as plain traits or powers. On first load, a migration checks the matching box by feat name (French, English, German, Russian):

- a trait or power named "Dual Nature" gets **Dual nature**;
- a trait or power named "Materialization" or "Astral Form" gets **Astral being**.

This covers world actors, unlinked tokens placed on scenes, world items and world compendiums. Module compendiums are not updated, and a feat with another name has to be checked by hand.

### Known limits

- Hiding an astral form is done on each client's display: the token still shows in the combat tracker.
- With an unlinked token, the astral form of a projection is an independent copy: damage taken by the form is not carried back to the body.
