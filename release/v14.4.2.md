# Release v14.4.2

## Fix — Shotguns specialization merged into Rifles

"Spec: Shotguns" does not exist in the rules: shotguns fall under the **Rifles** specialization.

- The Shotguns weapon type now uses Spec: Rifles.
- The Shotguns specialization is removed from the compendiums (EN, FR, DE) and from the NPC generator.

A migration updates existing worlds on first load:

- A character with Shotguns but not Rifles: the Shotguns specialization becomes **Rifles** (same item, nothing is lost).
- A character with both: the duplicate Shotguns specialization is removed.
- RR on Shotguns (feats, contacts) and custom weapons linked to Shotguns now point to Rifles.

This covers world actors, world items and world compendiums. Unlinked tokens placed on scenes are not updated.
